# Pendo Novus

- `verify-impact` decides whether exposed work produced the intended outcome and what should happen next.
- `verify-instrumentation` checks whether product data for a feature, flow, goal, or decision is trustworthy.
- `stress-test-plan` finds plan-breaking assumptions and returns a stronger first slice before coding.
- `ux-review` reviews local, unmerged changes for UX problems and backs its findings with Novus product analytics.
- `whats-next` compares current engineering work with product plans, shipping state, and customer evidence to recommend one next move.
- `build-investment` compares planned, built, and experienced investment across product areas to recommend one portfolio bet.
- `goal-to-experiment` is the downstream step for either decision skill: it turns a selected goal, portfolio bet, or bounded move into a reversible engineering experiment and evaluation plan. `build-investment` and `whats-next` are alternative entry points, not mandatory sequential steps.

The skills use the `novus` MCP server. Invoke them directly or ask naturally for an impact check, instrumentation check, plan stress test, UX review, next-work decision, portfolio investment review, or experiment brief.
